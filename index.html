Who We Are

Small-town service, full-line selection.

"We offer warmth and personal caring not available with national companies, along with expertise and selection most stores can't offer."

For decades, Quattlebaum Music has carried national brand names at competitive prices — and every customer still gets treated like a neighbor. We believe in personal, one-on-one service and proudly support local music education.

Sound reinforcement has always been a cornerstone of our business. Schools, churches, and athletic programs across the region rely on our no-nonsense evaluations, installations, and after-sale support. References available on request.
