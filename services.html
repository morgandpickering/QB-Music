Services

We're available for any music need.

Lessons, sound systems, and repairs — handled by people who've been doing it for decades.

01

Music Education

Our staff of trained and experienced instructors offer classes on a variety of instruments. Private 30-minute sessions are available Monday through Saturday.

02

Sound Evaluations & Proposals

40 years of experience evaluating and selling sound and public-address systems for schools, churches, and athletic stadiums. Free evaluations for new systems.

03

Sound Equipment Installation

Our 40 years with sound and PA systems includes full installation — let our technicians set you up for success.

04

General Service

Our experienced technicians can help with the adjustment and balance of sound systems. We don't employ in-house electronics techs, but we'll send your equipment to the dealer for repair and loan you an equivalent piece at no charge.

05

General Maintenance

Restringing, neck adjustments, set-ups, and tremolo balancing for electric and acoustic instruments, plus pickup installation. Free guitar stringing on every guitar purchased from us. We'll also help process warranty claims.

06

Orchestra Maintenance

We repair most string instruments in-store. Major repairs are sent to a trusted shop in Conway.
